# Email E-09

- **Source:** SpamAssassin corpus, row 5454
- **Subject:** $99 for 5 Nights in Magical Orlando with 2 Disney Tickets
- **Classification:** Suspicious
- **Type:** Unsolicited bulk promotion

## Indicators

- It comes from an unknown sender (famtriptravel[.]com) and was not requested.
- The deal is unusually cheap for what is offered, which is a classic too-good-to-be-true sign.
- It uses heavy capital letters and repeated exclamation marks.
- It is a generic bulk offer with no personal details.

## Why this classification

It does not ask for a password or payment details directly, so it is not clearly phishing. But unsolicited, very cheap travel offers are often used for fake bookings or hidden fees, so it should be treated with caution.

## Recommended action

Do not click links. If interested, find the company through its official website and check the offer there.
