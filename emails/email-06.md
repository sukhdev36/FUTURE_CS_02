# Email E-06

- **Source:** Kaggle phishing email dataset (phishing_email.csv), row 42327
- **Subject:** Weekend offer from a web hosting company
- **Classification:** Safe
- **Type:** Legitimate marketing email

## Indicators

- A single promotional offer from a well-known brand, with a discount code and clear terms.
- It does not ask for a login, password, payment details or attachment.
- It includes unsubscribe instructions and a postal address.
- It even includes advice on how to verify legitimate emails and spot email fraud.

## Why this classification

The content looks like ordinary marketing. There is no pressure and no request for sensitive information.

## Recommended action

No special action needed. Content alone cannot prove authenticity, so for a real inbox the sender should still be checked through the email headers.
